# Lino Art Shop — Design Ideas

<response>
<idea>
**Design Movement:** Industrial Gothic meets Printmaking Studio
**Core Principles:**
- Raw, tactile textures that echo the physical act of carving and pressing lino
- High contrast monochrome base with a single warm amber/ochre accent
- Asymmetric editorial layouts inspired by zine culture
- Typography as graphic element — large, bold display type used structurally

**Color Philosophy:**
- Background: near-black charcoal (#0e0e0e) — feels like ink on paper
- Surface: dark slate (#1a1a1a) for cards
- Accent: warm ochre/amber (#c8973a) — echoes the warmth of lino block material
- Text: off-white (#f0ece4) — aged paper feel, not harsh white

**Layout Paradigm:**
- Staggered product grid with varying card heights (masonry-style)
- Full-bleed hero with large overlapping type
- Left-rail navigation on desktop, drawer on mobile
- Generous whitespace between sections to let prints breathe

**Signature Elements:**
- Rough ink-stroke SVG dividers between sections
- Subtle grain/noise texture overlay on all backgrounds
- Stamp-style badges for product categories

**Interaction Philosophy:**
- Hover reveals: product details slide up from bottom of card
- Cart drawer slides in from right with inky fade
- Smooth page transitions with slight vertical drift

**Animation:**
- Entrance animations: elements fade up with 0.3s stagger
- Hover: image scales 1.03x with shadow deepening
- Cart badge: subtle bounce on item add

**Typography System:**
- Display: "Playfair Display" (serif, bold) — editorial gravitas
- Body: "DM Sans" (sans-serif, regular/medium) — clean legibility
- Accent labels: "DM Mono" (monospace) — price tags, edition numbers
</idea>

**Color Philosophy:**
- Background: pure black (#000000)

- Text: stark white (#ffffff)

- Rigid column grid with thick rule lines as dividers
- Product names in oversized uppercase spanning full width
- Price and edition info in small monospace beneath
- No rounded corners anywhere

**Signature Elements:**
- Thick horizontal rules as section dividers
- Uppercase tracking-widest labels
- Numbered edition stamps in red

**Interaction Philosophy:**
- Instant, no-transition state changes — brutally direct
- Hover inverts colors (black bg → white bg, white text → black text)
- No animations except essential loading states

**Animation:**
- Minimal: only opacity fades for modals/drawers
- Hover: immediate color inversion, no easing

**Typography System:**
- Display: "Space Grotesk" (bold, uppercase)
- Body: "Space Mono" (monospace)
- All caps for headings, sentence case for body

## Chosen Design: Industrial Gothic meets Printmaking Studio (Option 1)

Warm ochre accent on near-black charcoal, Playfair Display + DM Sans typography, masonry-style product grid, grain texture overlays, and ink-stroke aesthetic throughout.
